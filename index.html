N5 Life – Community Guidelines

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

N5 Life is designed to help customers and service professionals connect in a respectful, safe and trustworthy environment. These Community Guidelines apply to everyone using the N5 Life platform.

1. Be Respectful

Treat customers, workers and other users with respect. Harassment, abusive language, threats, bullying or discriminatory behaviour is not permitted.

2. Provide Accurate Information

Users should provide genuine and accurate information about themselves, their services, experience and other details requested by N5 Life.

3. No Fraud or Misleading Information

Users must not create fake profiles, impersonate another person, submit false documents or provide misleading information to other users.

4. Professional Behaviour

Service professionals are expected to communicate professionally, respect customer property and provide services honestly.

5. No Unlawful Activity

N5 Life must not be used to arrange, promote or participate in illegal activities.

6. Protect Personal Information

Users should respect the privacy of others and must not misuse, publish or share another person's personal information without permission.

7. Reviews and Ratings

Reviews and ratings should be honest and based on genuine experiences. Fake reviews, review manipulation, threats or attempts to unfairly influence ratings are not permitted.

8. Reporting Problems

Users are encouraged to report suspicious, abusive, unsafe or inappropriate behaviour to N5 Life so that appropriate action can be considered.

9. Enforcement

N5 Life may investigate reported violations and may restrict, suspend or terminate accounts that violate these guidelines or applicable laws.
